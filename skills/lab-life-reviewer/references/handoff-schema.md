# Life-review handoff schema

The handoff is the durable contract between the interview task and the archive task. It must be detailed enough that the second task does not need the full chat context, while remaining faithful to Raw.

## Raw record

Include:

- interview date, topic, approximate time range, mode, and status;
- allowed use, prohibited use, and privacy level;
- transcript or detailed chronological account in the user's order;
- questions and answers where recoverable;
- additions, corrections, false starts, uncertain terms, transcript gaps, and confirmed background noise exclusions;
- links or paths to related materials inspected.

Do not polish Raw into a coherent retrospective essay. Preserve the evidence trail.

## Structured handoff

Use the following sections when applicable. Keep substantial narrative detail and concrete examples; do not fill a section with invented material merely to complete the template.

1. **Scope and status** — event, period, interview coverage, completion state, use permission, privacy.
2. **Continuity checkpoint** — prior archived/handoff node, current node, and next waiting node.
3. **Detailed chronology** — sequence, approximate dates, corrections, and confidence.
4. **Event or project cards** — context/problem, user's role, actions, other people's roles, decisions, constraints, result, aftermath.
5. **Explicit first-person factual claims** — marked as first-person account unless externally verified.
6. **Feelings and self-explanations** — retain the user's own language and avoid upgrading them to facts.
7. **Related materials and external evidence** — what each item directly supports, does not support, and its path.
8. **Failures, achievements, costs, and turning points** — include both success and negative evidence.
9. **Corrections, uncertainty, contradictions, and role boundaries**.
10. **AI candidate hypotheses** — basis, competing explanation, counterexample or missing evidence, and confirmation status.
11. **Privacy and use restrictions** — public, internal, decision/review only, or excluded from long-term context.
12. **Archive recommendations** — candidates for Raw/source/project/decision/method/synthesis/concept or no ingestion; these are recommendations, not approved changes.
13. **Next-session resume note** — one detailed paragraph enabling the interview task to continue without reconstructing the whole conversation.
14. **Small set of next questions** — only unresolved questions that would materially change the account.

## Quality checks

Before handoff:

- verify that the most recent correction controls the current timeline;
- preserve approximate language for uncertain dates, counts, scores, titles, and names;
- distinguish user action from team action;
- distinguish outcome from the user's explanation of why it happened;
- include relevant details that would be lost in a compressed timeline;
- state whether materials were actually inspected or merely mentioned;
- state whether the topic is complete enough for archival or still an interim package.
